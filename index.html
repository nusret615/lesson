Steve Jobs Biography

Birthday:February 24, 1955
Nationality:American
Born In:San Francisco
Died On:October 5, 2011
Cause Of Death:Cancer
Founder/Co-Founder:Apple Inc, Pixar Animation Studios, NeXT Computer, Inc
Discoveries/InventionsiPod, iPhone, iPad, Macintosh
Popularly known as the Father of the Digital World, Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur, investor, and co-founder of Apple Inc. He helped transform consumer electronics with innovative products such as the iMac, iPod, iPhone, and iPad. He also founded Pixar Animation Studios and NeXT Inc. Jobs was known for his creativity and unique ideas. Although he attended college, he dropped out after a short time to pursue his interests.

Childhood and Early Life

Born on February 24, 1955, Steve Paul Jobs was adopted by Paul Reinhold Jobs and Clara Jobs. His biological parents were Abdulfattah Jandali and Joanne Carole Schieble.
From a young age, Jobs was interested in mechanics. He spent many hours with his father dismantling and rebuilding electronic devices in the family garage.
After graduating from high school in 1972, he enrolled at Reed College but dropped out after six months to pursue creative classes, including calligraphy.

Family and Personal Life

Steve Jobs had a daughter named Lisa Brennan-Jobs in 1978. He later married Laurene Powell in 1991, and they had three children: Reed, Erin, and Eve.
