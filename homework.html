Bank of Russia statistics

Number of banks in Russia from 2001 to 2020

According to the statistics of the Bank of Russia, the number of banks in the Russian Federation as of 01.01.2020 is 484, that is, over the past year their number has decreased by 42 (484 - 442 = 42). If we take statistics for the last 10 years, then their number has decreased by more than half.

The total number of operating banks in Russia in the context of Federal Districts and in Moscow for 2018 and 2019 looks like this:

No. Number of operating banks by regions of Russia 01/01/2019 01/01/2020

2 NORTH-WESTERN FEDERAL DISTRICT 41 37

The table shows that in 2019 the reduction in the number of banks in the Russian Federation continued, affecting 7 out of 8 federal districts. The downward trend in banks continued in 2020.
